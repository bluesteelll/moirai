## Notes

### cycle.e64mib (run 1)

- **The ruled quantity** is the back-to-back CLI commit cycle: each sample follows the previous cycle at once, about
  15 ms apart, so each cycle carries some cost over from the one before (R-38, R-39).
- **Spaced commits, a sensitivity.** A cycle that follows about 30 ms of other work (the first sample of each block,
  after 100 floor flushes) has a p99 of about 17 ms (est.: 17.073 ms, the median over the 5 repetitions of 100 such
  samples each, below t2's n of 1,000). It is not the ruled quantity, and it does not change the decision (R-39).
- **Without each block's first sample** (R-34 M-4) the cycle's p99 is 37.333 ms and the floor's 0.361 ms; the verdict
  is unchanged. Both arms have a batch of 1, so the per-operation p99 bound is the recorded p99 (M-6), and neither p50
  is under 20 × the timer's resolution (M-5).
- **Typical commit sizes only**, 300-600 B (`m1.rs:26`, R-31 Q2), not an owner's real mix, which does not exist yet.
  The size tail is measurement 2's.
- **A proxy.** The cycle runs the toy log with the production profile, not the product engine. The toy's open replays
  its whole store generation, and the open, the directory flushes and the close are not split out.
- **Activity outside the checks of MP §2.3** fell in the run: a 78 ms AppX registration repair and one sppsvc run. It
  bars nothing (R-39).
- **The process readings** include the driver's setup and the samples the runner holds (R-34 M-9). They are not gated.

## Decision for WP-81a

**Leader in M1, with CLI forwarding.** AR:140's trigger, CLI commits over 20 ms, fires: `cycle.e64mib`'s p99 is
37.2 ms against 20 ms (1.86 times the budget), at gate tier t2, the median over 5 repetitions of 10,000 cycles; every
repetition's p99 lies in 36.1-41.8 ms. The leader goes into M1 with the CLI forwarding to it, which adds 3-4 units to
M1 and to every later date (60:913).

- **The spaced cadence** (about 17 ms p99, est.) does not change the decision: measurement 2's last ack and writer
  wait fire the trigger on their own grounds, and measurement 1 is a trigger in its own right (AR:140, 60:757).
- **The saving per CLI commit** is an upper estimate, not a bound, before the leader's IPC: about 14 ms at p50 and
  37 ms at p99 back to back, and about 9 ms and 17 ms for spaced commits (est.).
- **G8, the Defender close cost, is unmeasured** (`close.e64mib` left no record), so the share of the cycle that
  Defender's close causes is unknown.

**The basis.**

- One idle run on the laptop, at commit c4de9c7; the night's records stand at u16's merge (R-38, R-40).
- No loaded run yet (V5). Load only adds time, so the decision does not wait for it.
- The checks after the night (MP §2.3; R-34 M-1, M-3, M-17) set nothing aside in measurement 1.
- The idle condition admits the resident supervising agent session, its desktop app and the owner's resident
  applications at rest (MP §2.3, R-34 B-2).
- The commit sizes are synthetic typical sizes, not an owner's real mix (R-24 Q10, R-31 Q2).
