## Notes

### last-ack.c100 (run 9)

- **The deciding run**, with the design's cost body (B) (R-31 Q1). Its p99 misses in every repetition: 175.7-203.2 ms
  against 50 ms.
- **The gate tier is t3**, which gates p95 and the maximum (MP §3.3). The criterion names p99, and the module doc says
  t2 (`m2.rs:5`, a Minor for the next unit that changes `m2.rs`). The verdict is the same under p95, p99 or the
  maximum.
- **Without each block's first sample** (R-34 M-4) the p99 is 187.8 ms and the p95 142.7 ms. The batch is 1, so the
  per-operation p99 bound is the recorded p99 (M-6), and no p50 is under 20 × the timer's resolution (M-5).
- **The toy-open proxy.** Each child's commit opens a fresh toy store and replays about 2.2 MB of its generation:
  phase 1 has a p50 of 9.6 ms, a p99 of 25.9 ms and a maximum of 36.4 ms (driver-reported, not in the record). Without
  that direct share the p99 stays at 151.4 ms or more (est.). The indirect share, 16 replays contending with the
  appends, flushes and hand-offs, is not separated. The product's open budget is 3 ms or less.
- **Activity outside the checks of MP §2.3:** 34 WER re-reports of old queued reports, about 1 s of the 471 s run. They
  bar nothing (R-39).

### last-ack.a100 (run 7)

- **R-31's sensitivity run**, with the uniform cost body (A): p99 191.8 ms. It agrees with c100 on "leader in", so
  R-31 raises no owner question.
- It is a separate run, made just after c100 and not interleaved with it, so the two are not a paired difference.
- Defender's hourly health reports fell in it; they are not scans.

### writer-wait.c100 (run 12)

- **T2's value** (R-24 Q7): every writer grant of a burst, the flush holder's re-acquisitions included.
- Its 10,000 values a repetition come from about 300 bursts, so they are correlated and the tail's effective sample
  size is smaller than n. The verdict rests on the spread over the repetitions, 69.3-74.2 ms at p99.
- Each block's first sample is an append grant. Those 500 samples, an estimate of the append-only wait, have a p99 of
  82.2 ms (est.).
- The toy-open caveat of `last-ack.c100` applies.

## Decision for WP-81a

**Leader in M1** (a1-dispositions.md:185). It is provisional only in that M1's gates re-check it. At the maximum
injected in-lock cost, the 16-writer last acknowledgement misses the 50 ms bound under both cost bodies (R-31):

| Statistic | (B) design body, `last-ack.c100` | (A) uniform body, `last-ack.a100` |
|---|---|---|
| p99, the criterion | 187.8 ms [175.7-203.2] | 191.8 ms [175.5-216.1] |
| p95, gated at t3 | 142.5 ms | 151.8 ms |
| max, gated at t3 | 309.6 ms | 266.5 ms |

- **(A) and (B) agree**, so the decision does not depend on the in-lock cost's body. It does not depend on the cost at
  all: with no injected cost the p99 is 155.4 ms.
- **T2.** The 16-writer writer wait's p99 is 72.0 ms against 50 ms, so AR:140's T2 trigger fires in the toy.
- **Flushes per burst** reach a maximum of 5 against 3 or fewer (AR:1797); p95 is 3.
- **The 2 s bounds hold** (G1): the largest values are a last ack of 494.3 ms, a writer wait of 324.0 ms and a
  hand-off of 137.2 ms.
- **Fairness and the hand-off** are observations: a median of 37 of 120 pairs overtaken, and a hand-off p99 of
  14.0 ms.
- **The toy-open caveat.** The toy's fresh open per commit replays about 2.2 MB of its generation, and the product may
  read less. Without the replay's direct share the p99 stays at 151.4 ms or more (est.), but the indirect contention
  cost is not separated.

**The basis.**

- One idle run per quantity on the laptop, at commit c4de9c7; the night's records stand at u16's merge (R-38, R-40).
- No loaded run yet (V5). The idle runs already miss, so the guard margin does not arise.
- The checks after the night (MP §2.3; R-34 M-1, M-3, M-17) set nothing aside in measurement 2.
- The idle condition admits the resident supervising agent session, its desktop app and the owner's resident
  applications at rest (MP §2.3, R-34 B-2).
- The commit sizes are drawn from the full spec-derived size distribution (`m2.rs:12`), not from an owner's real mix,
  which does not exist yet (R-24 Q10).
- The injected in-lock costs follow AR:1717 and AR:1798 (R-31 Q1).
- The process readings are the coordinator's only, setup included; the 16 children's memory is not recorded
  (R-34 M-9).
